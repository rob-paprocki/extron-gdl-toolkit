test: read seeds and templates from the repo, and audit the whole corpus

tests/_corpus.py resolves the seeds and Extron's templates from seeds/
and vendor/ first and the GUI Designer install second, and treats an
un-pulled LFS pointer as absent. The three tests that hard-coded C:
paths now run wherever the files are and skip cleanly where they are
not. 157 pass; the 27 seed and template tests ran against the repo's
own copies.

tests/audit_corpus.py re-tests the repo's documented beliefs against
every project it can reach: 6 fixtures, 14 seeds, 44 templates. Its
first version loaded all of them before checking anything and helped
push the box past its 8.5 GB commit limit; it now reduces each project
to a small dict of facts and releases it before opening the next.

First full run, 9 of 12 held. The three that did not:

- Arial Black is declared but not embedded in 11 of the 14 seeds, with
  no sfnt table anywhere near its declared 119,904 bytes; the
  Turbulence seed declares Arial the same way and the Shockwave seeds
  extron_shockwave.ttf. True of every fixture, false of a fresh
  project.
- MODELS_FULL has TLP1230WTG at 800x480 and 0 DPI - the generic
  platform class's default. Extron's own 1230W project builds at
  1920x720, its only supported resolution. Separately, the table holds
  one orientation per model while the platforms support both (300M
  320x480 and 480x320; 1035M 1280x800 and 800x1280).
- Build's full-canvas popup references are one per modal popup, the
  Offline Page counting only when EnableOfflinePage is on. The
  documented rule ("one per modal popup") was off by one on 19 of the
  20 built projects; "all but the Offline Page" then failed only the
  Turbulence seed, the one with the offline page enabled. The audit now
  encodes that rule and it holds on all 20.

The first two are recorded in docs/ROADMAP.md rather than patched here.

Also: New-ImageProbe.ps1 defaults to this repo and the vendor/ icon kit
instead of the old Parallels share, and clean-room.json names its seed.
